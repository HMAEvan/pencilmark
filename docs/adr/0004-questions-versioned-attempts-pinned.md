# Questions are separate from Attempts, and Attempts are pinned to a Question revision

Questions live in a bank that belongs to no Set. A change to anything shown during answering, or to how the Question is graded, creates a new revision, and earlier revisions are kept. Each Attempt records the revision it was made against, and is displayed and graded by that revision. Otherwise, fixing a wrong answer key would silently regrade, or contradict, past Sessions.
